# Cassian 1.8.1 Preview — one complete amp head

Tone now shows a complete amplifier: carry handle, covered grille, metal front panel, continuous enclosure and feet. Six working knobs, channel switch, wolf logo and power jewel sit directly on the photographed panel. The four head-selection cards are removed. Choose a complete rig from the header's Preset menu; the head artwork follows its sound family.

Lumen retains ivory and nickel, Rubicon oxblood and brass, Ferrum graphite and steel, and Aurelia walnut and linen. Each has its own live wordmark. The whole photograph scales without cropping or a separate CSS panel. The tuner overlays the grille and leaves the controls accessible. Wolf branding remains on metal without a ring; the actual engine/capture identity remains below the head.

Practice, Board and Takes retain compact heads. Real Drive, tone, ambience/Presence and Master controls keep their parameter and automation identities. Preset recall remains complete-rig recall and is disabled during loading, recording or review playback. Save edits before changing rigs. This update changes presentation and recall controls, not audio processing or capture availability.

The four revised WebP assets are bundled into the offline editor; there is no runtime image service. UI regressions cover preset-driven artwork, complete recall, recording guards and the six working panel controls. Visual checks cover all four heads and workspaces at the supported minimum 860 × 620 and larger sizes.
