# Cassian product roadmap

Checkpoint: 2026-10-09. Current feature preview: **1.8.0**, with cabinet route/comparison tools and expanded impulse timing coverage, following four custom covered amplifier heads and first-session route guidance. See the [versioned path to 2.0.0](ROAD-TO-2.0.md). The product is a guitar workstation and VST3 processor, with practice, recording and reamping; a general multitrack DAW is outside the current scope. The owner reports practical sound tests tried so far are passing. That is useful playing feedback, not an automatic certification of every interface, host or preset.

## What should make Cassian worth choosing

The owner wants a fairly priced alternative to recurring software costs, using open-source code with optional paid installers/support. Installed access should not depend on a recurring subscription. Existing user content is stored locally and portable; original source is AGPL-3.0-or-later. Final prices, support duration and update entitlements are business decisions still to be specified. No new billing, cloud account, telemetry or licensing service is added by this roadmap.

1. **Good tones immediately, including cleans.** Ship an original or explicitly cleared starting collection that works without downloading a first capture. Test tight rhythm, singing lead, crunch, expressive clean and ambient variants with consistent listening levels. Keep actual capture dependencies visible. The private 103-file owner bank is not a publicly cleared factory bank.
2. **Play, record and finish a usable take in one place.** Refine backing balance, dry-DI protection, reamp/version comparison and guitar/video WAV export. Reusable practice sets/history now organize elapsed work, notes, exact recording links and local progress. Use planner feedback to refine those views. Avoid turning normal playing into a complicated setup sequence.
3. **Keep the player's work portable.** Preserve rigs, sounds and takes across upgrades and explicit transfers; explain missing dependencies and recover interruptions without overwriting originals. Avoid account-dependent access to local recordings and tones.
4. **Make the first five minutes easy.** Test a beginner opening the installer, choosing an interface/input, hearing a starter rig, saving an edit and recording/exporting audio. Improve calibration guidance and visible diagnostics where these trials show confusion.
5. **Demonstrate sound and reliability.** Use recorded clean/rhythm/lead DI fixtures and level-matched listening comparisons, sustained interface runs, actual DAW recall/automation and fresh-PC tests. Automated tests support these claims; they do not prove superior sound or establish willingness to pay.

## Next order

Recording recovery/download cleanup, guided input setup, the original starter-tone guide, bounded normal-speed long-WAV review/loop prefetch, reusable local practice sets/history, recording links and progress are implemented through 1.6.0 Preview. The next implementation milestone is first-session polish; see the scoped 1.7–2.0 sequence in [Path to 2.0.0](ROAD-TO-2.0.md). Prioritize beginner setup/listening trials, sustained recording/review and planner acceptance, and second-PC transfers alongside that work. Collect the owner's next practical tests after restringing, retaining exact rig/interface/driver/rate/buffer settings when available. Complete the manual release evidence and cleared sound distribution alongside these improvements.

Pitch transpose/harmonizers, parallel amps, cabinet alignment and smoother switching are later DSP milestones with their own latency, recall and performance work. The promotional/community website remains deferred until the app is more mature. It should eventually offer demos, simple current-version downloads, documentation, transparent roadmap and suggestions, with repository-backed participation.

There is no defensible percentage for sound parity or paid-release readiness. Feature breadth is established by the code; audible quality, beginner success and commercial readiness need their own evidence. Signing remains deferred at the owner's request, and pending acceptance fields stay pending.
