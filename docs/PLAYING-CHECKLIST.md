# Practical playing check

Use this after restringing and tuning. Results remain unrecorded until a player actually performs the exercise. The app's Help & setup support report can supply the version, interface, driver, input, rate/buffer and counters. Review before sharing; nothing is submitted automatically.

## A short pass

1. **Input first.** Run the eight-second check with your hardest playing. Lower physical gain and repeat if clipped; a software gate cannot repair that. Start at a comfortable listening level.
2. **Clean dynamics.** Use Prism Clean. Play quiet/loud arpeggios, open chords and held notes; check attack, separation and decay. Try your normal guitar-volume range.
3. **Crunch and lead.** Use Copper Blues, then Velvet Lead. Check bends, alternate picking and legato, including how effects sit behind the notes. These original starter rigs load without captures.
4. **Heavy rhythm.** Use Iron Rhythm, then your preferred imported high-gain rig. Check palm mutes, chord definition, held notes and silence between phrases. Record whether crackle occurs over notes, while idle, or during a change, alongside counter changes.
5. **Backing and click.** Try browser backing through your usual outputs, then an imported Practice track and the metronome. Check clarity without raising levels sharply. Browser audio cannot become a Cassian backing stem.
6. **Save and return.** Save a complete rig, change it, reopen it and check the amp, cabinet, pedals and scenes. Input trim and listening controls should remain where you set them.
7. **Record and export.** Record dry/processed/backing stems, review and reamp, then export a guitar or mixed soundtrack WAV. Check the separate export balance. Change Play Along listening controls and confirm they do not change recorded/reamped audio.
8. **Longer run.** Play for a sustained session with your demanding board and intended buffer. Keep the duration, beginning/end counters, switching/recording exercises and outcome. Test a second supported buffer when available.
