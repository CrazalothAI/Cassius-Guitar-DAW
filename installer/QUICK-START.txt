CASSIAN

Open Cassian from the Windows Start menu, your desktop shortcut, or Cassian.exe.
The installer places the app in your user profile; administrator access is not required.

FIRST SOUND
1. Connect your guitar and headphones/speakers to your audio interface.
2. Open Audio settings in Cassian. Choose your interface, guitar input and outputs.
3. Select Warm Jazz or Modern Metalcore under Cassian built-in rigs. These need no additional files.
4. Raise Master gradually. Keep the interface input gain below clipping.
5. Use your interface's Playback monitoring to avoid doubling the dry guitar.

PRACTICE
Open Practice to load a backing track, change speed without changing pitch,
see its waveform, save named loop sections, and record dry/processed guitar takes.
Open Takes to review, name, favorite and reamp your recordings.
New takes also save a synchronized backing stem from tracks loaded in Practice.
In Takes, expand Video soundtrack, choose the take version and balance, then Export for video.
Import the 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV into Clipchamp alongside your camera video.
Browser/YouTube playback and metronome/count-in clicks are not recorded into the soundtrack.

YOUR RIG
Tone shows the full amp head; Board opens all detailed stages below a compact strip.
Save as names a complete rig. Save updates it; Edited shows tone changes.
Library imports NAM amp/pedal captures and WAV cabinets. Performance sets MIDI foot control.

PLAY ALONG
Open Mix and try Bring guitar forward for listening-only balance and focus.
With YouTube/browser backing, start video volume around 25% and adjust to taste.
These controls keep dry/processed recordings and offline reamps unchanged.

OPTIONAL DAW PLUGIN
The installer can also install the VST3 plugin for the current user at:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\VST3\Cassian.vst3
Rescan plugins in your DAW. If needed, add that VST3 location to its scan paths.

UPDATES AND UNINSTALLING
Close Cassian and any DAW using its plugin, then run the newer setup EXE to update.
Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps > Cassian.
Your saved settings, imported captures, rigs, practice sections and recordings are retained.

PORTABLE DOWNLOAD
If using Cassian-Windows.zip, extract all files first, then open Cassian.exe at the top
of the extracted folder. The editor needs Microsoft's WebView2 Evergreen Runtime.

When a licensed sound bank is supplied, keep the Sounds folder beside the portable app.
The installer places that bank automatically; missing capture recipes name their required files.
