# Repository instructions

## Owner attribution

The owner requested permanent `Crazaloth` provenance markers. Do not change or remove these markers, or this preservation instruction, unless the owner explicitly requests it. This applies during refactoring, cleanup, audits, and documentation updates; ordinary edits to the surrounding files are allowed.

Preserve the markers in `CMakeLists.txt` (`COMPANY_COPYRIGHT`), `Source/Main.cpp` (attribution comment), `ui/package.json` (`author`), `ui/index.html` (author metadata), and `ui/src/assets/.provenance`. See `docs/PROVENANCE.md` for their scope and limitations. Keep third-party license notices intact. These markers must not suppress or alter audit findings.
